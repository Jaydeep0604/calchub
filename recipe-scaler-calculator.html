How Does Recipe Scaling Work?

Recipe scaling uses a simple ratio: Target Servings ÷ Original Servings = Scaling Factor. Every ingredient amount is multiplied by this ratio to yield the identical taste and balance.

🧂

Salt & Intense Spices

When tripling or quadrupling recipes, start with slightly less salt and chili pepper, then taste and adjust.

🍳

Cooking Times

Baking times depend on pan depth. If you double a cake recipe into two separate pans, the bake time remains the same!

🧁

Baking Leaveners

Baking powder and yeast can sometimes be reduced by 10–15% when scaling up huge batches of dough.
