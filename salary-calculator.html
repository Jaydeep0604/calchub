How Hourly Wages Convert to Annual Salary

In standard full-time employment, an employee works 40 hours per week for 52 weeks a year, totaling 2,080 working hours annually. As a quick rule of thumb: double your hourly rate and add three zeros to get your approximate yearly salary (e.g., $35/hr ≈ $70,000–$72,800/yr).

🗓️

Standard Full-Time (2,080 hrs)

40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 paid hours. If you receive 2 weeks unpaid leave, use 50 weeks instead.

⏱️

Overtime & 1.5x Multiplier

Non-exempt workers working beyond 40 hours per week are typically entitled to 1.5x time-and-a-half pay.

📑

Net vs Gross Pay

This calculator shows gross earnings before income tax, social security, health insurance, and 401(k) deductions.
