Mastering Everyday Percentage Math

Percentages appear constantly in everyday life—from sales discounts and restaurant bills to stock market gains and pay raises. Here are 3 mental math secrets to calculate percentages instantly.

💡

The Reversible Percentage Trick

x% of y = y% of x. Need 16% of 50? Just calculate 50% of 16 instead, which is easily 8!

🏷️

Fast Coupon Discount

To find 20% off, multiply by 0.8. To find 30% off, multiply by 0.7. For 15%, find 10% and add half of that number.

📊

Percentage Change vs Points

If interest rates rise from 4% to 5%, that is a 1 percentage point increase, but a 25% relative increase!
