How Does Fuel Cost Calculation Work?

Planning a road trip with friends or budgeting your daily office drive? Calculating fuel cost requires three straightforward variables:

1

Determine Volume Needed

Divide distance by your vehicle efficiency (e.g., 120 miles ÷ 28 MPG = 4.29 gallons; or 200 km ÷ 14 km/L = 14.28 Liters).

2

Multiply by Pump Price

Multiply volume by price per gallon or liter (e.g., 4.29 gal × $3.50 = $15.00 total trip fuel).

3

Split Fairly with Carpool

Divide the total cost by the number of friends in the car so everyone chips in an exact fair share.
